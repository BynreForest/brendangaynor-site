Fit Tunes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Fit Tunes does not collect personal information.

Your playlists

The app reads your Apple Music / iTunes playlists on your device only to display songs and your saved cues. This data is not sent to the developer.

Your cues

Cues you write are stored on your device.

Crash diagnostics

Anonymous crash diagnostics may be collected to improve app stability. They are not linked to your identity.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.
