About

Cue is a personalized concierge platform that connects businesses with their customers. Users scan a QR code, tap an NFC tag, or follow a private link and are routed straight to what they need: on-demand support, information, guided training, scheduling a call, or placing an order. It’s fully brandable and works without requiring an app download.

AI triages incoming requests and gives instant answers, then hands off to a real person when needed. An AI Voice feature, powered by ElevenLabs and Bland.ai, lets customers talk to Cue naturally. Cue adapts to different industries, including pharma, hospitality, retail, and finance.

I built and maintain the iOS app, first released in 2022 and now on version 2.4. Work includes AI-driven request triage, the AI Voice integration, a bidirectional WKWebView JavaScript bridge between native and web content, timezone-aware meeting scheduling, and API integrations for routing.
