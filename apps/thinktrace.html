About

Thinktrace is an iOS voice-notes app. Record voice memos, then ask questions about what you said and get answers drawn from your own recordings.

Recordings are transcribed on-device with Apple’s SpeechAnalyzer. Questions go to a Python/FastAPI backend, where a LangGraph agent pipeline — a router, a retriever, and an answer composer — searches your notes using Amazon Bedrock Titan Text Embeddings V2 and PostgreSQL with pgvector.

Answer quality is measured with an eval suite built on a golden Q&A set, and the app is tested with Swift Testing.
