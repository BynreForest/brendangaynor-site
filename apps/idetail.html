About

iDetail is a content delivery platform for life science enterprise teams. Sales reps use it on iPad to present and share approved content with real-time sync and closed-loop marketing tracking.

As iOS Lead at Thingee, I owned the app’s full lifecycle for over 10 years: architecture, development, releases, and support, shipping through both Enterprise and App Store distribution.

Key features: automatic content downloads and expiration, update notifications, online and offline access, presenting and sharing approved content, custom branding and themes, metadata filtering, real-time content-usage tracking, and controlling presentations on up to 7 additional iPads.
