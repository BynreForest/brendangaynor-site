About

Fit Tunes is built for fitness instructors who teach to music. Instead of juggling notecards, instructors attach workout and interval cues directly to each song in their existing Apple Music / iTunes playlists, and those cues appear on screen whenever the song plays in class.

It works with the playlists you already have (nothing to import or rebuild), shows album artwork, and respects playlist settings like shuffle and repeat.

First released in 2016, Fit Tunes was redesigned in version 2.0 with a new playlist grid and now-playing screen, Dark Mode support, and an improved landscape layout for use on a stand mid-class. Privacy-friendly: the app collects only anonymous crash diagnostics, not linked to the user.
